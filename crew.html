Meet the crew

The people who will know your name on day one.

Our teachers are working musicians and genuinely kind humans. They will meet you where you are, laugh at the wrong notes with you, and get you playing music you actually like.

Our founder

Adam B Young

Founder and director

Adam B Young founded the academy in 2012 with a simple belief: music is a powerful tool for personal growth. What began as a local dream has grown into a thriving community of students, families, and educators, serving ages 2 to 102.

He still greets students at the door, still teaches, and still believes the best thing about a music school is the people in it.

“Music gives people a voice, a confidence, and a brighter tomorrow.”

Adam B Young Founder, Milton Pace Music Academy

The teaching crew

One standard: everyone leaves better than they came in.

Kami Hawks

Piano & theory

Kami teaches piano and the theory behind it, from first notes to students who want to understand what they are playing. She is unhurried with nervous beginners and keeps a running list of songs her students actually want to learn.

  • Piano
  • Beginners
  • Ages 5+

Courtney Brooks

Music instructor, multiple instruments

Courtney teaches across several instruments, so students can start where they are curious and switch as that changes. She is patient with first timers and quick to find the song that makes practice feel worth it.

  • Multi-instrument
  • Beginners
  • All ages

Melia Coulter

Winds, brass & violin

Melia Coulter is a recent graduate from the University of West Florida, receiving her Bachelor’s Degree in Music Education with a focus in wind band instruments of all types, and has years of violin experience as well. During her student teaching at Hobbs Middle School, all band students received a Superior rating at the annual District 1 Solo & Ensemble. She is determined to help students find their path in music and experience the joy it can bring.

  • Winds & brass
  • Violin
  • Band

Logan Selner

Music instructor, multiple instruments

Logan teaches across several instruments, which makes him a good fit for students who are still working out what they want to play. He keeps lessons practical and gets people playing real songs early, long before the theory feels like homework.

  • Multi-instrument
  • Beginners
  • All ages

How we teach

Three things every teacher here agrees on

1

Start with the person

We find out what you want to play before we hand you a method book. Motivation beats discipline every time.

2

Progress you can hear

Clear goals, honest feedback, and real songs. You should be able to hear the difference month over month.

3

Nobody practices alone

Recitals, jam nights, bands and a lobby full of people who have been exactly where you are.
